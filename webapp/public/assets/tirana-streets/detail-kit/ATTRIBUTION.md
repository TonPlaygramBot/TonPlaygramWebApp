# Original urban fixture kit

All geometry and material designs in `urban-fixtures.glb` are original project-authored work, dedicated under CC0-1.0. No third-party textures, scans or branded models are included. The editable authoring script is `assets-source/tirana-detail-kit/build.mjs`, extending the existing original `recipes.mjs` modules.

These are Tirana-inspired architectural fixtures, not surveyed reproductions. The kit adds air-conditioner fan guards and service plumbing, ribbed water tanks, collector frames, vent grilles and window/door detail. It preserves the existing mapped-building placements. See `manifest.json` for SHA-256, size and included asset IDs.
