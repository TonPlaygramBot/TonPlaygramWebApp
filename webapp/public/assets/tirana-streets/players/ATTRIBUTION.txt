The supplied archive prohibits commercial use. Inclusion in a draft review does
not establish permission for a commercial release. See agent-47.license.txt for
the original notice. The supplied glTF contains a skeleton but no animation
clips or morph targets, despite the source title.

Modifications to all three supplied models: face color textures resized to at
most 2048px, body color maps to at most 1024px, soldier normal maps and material
data maps to at most 512px; normal vectors renormalized after resizing; JPEG/PNG compression;
source glTF/GLB repackaged as self-contained GLB.
Geometry, skin weights, skeleton hierarchy, node transforms, material settings
and animation data are preserved. Original uploads are not modified.

Reproduce with webapp/scripts/prepare-tirana-player-assets.py. Exact input and
output hashes, geometry counts and texture changes are recorded in asset-audit.json.
