The uploaded GLB identifies the **Sketchfab Standard licence** (https://sketchfab.com/licenses). It is not a CC0/open-source model. Confirm the supplied model's acquisition rights before distribution in a commercial release.

The uploader supplied both originals. Runtime derivatives bake transforms, resize textures (mosque up to 2048 px, car up to 1024 px), and use Draco with 16-bit position quantisation. Their original triangle topology is preserved. The mosque scan is centred and its main plinth aligned with terrain; below-plinth scan fragments are retained. Original SHA-256 and derived SHA-256 values are in manifest.json. Source scans include existing imperfections.

## Original bike models

City bicycle, mountain bike, delivery e-bike, city scooter and street motorcycle were authored for this repository in Blender. Editable .blend files and Python generators are included. These five original meshes may be used under **CC0 1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. They do not copy a downloaded third-party bike.
