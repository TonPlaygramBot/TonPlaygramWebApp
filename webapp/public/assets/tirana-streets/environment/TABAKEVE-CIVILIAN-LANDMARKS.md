# Tabakëve civilian landmarks (Tirana Streets solo)

- **Shkolla e Baletit / Shkolla Kombëtare Koreografike:** [OSM campus way 541840626](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/541840626), with [existing mapped school building 443993963](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/443993963). The [Albanian Central Election Commission's polling-place list](https://cec.org.al/Portals/0/Documents/CEC%202013/zgjedhje-vendore/2007/njesi_QV/QV_2007/report1/349.htm) confirms the school on Rruga Petro Nini Luarasi. The map label uses the existing Tabakëve/InstitutionLayer identity, footprint, and visually authored frontage; it does not add another school mesh, sign, entry or interior.
- **Mihal Grameno:** [OSM memorial node 9124938870](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/9124938870), in the retained neighbourhood source. [Top Channel's interview with sculptor Besart Papa](https://top-channel.tv/video/valltaret-nga-skulpture-publike-e-vandalizuar-ne-nje-nje-replike-unike-ne-shitje-artkand-2/) confirms the public bust at the start of Rruga Mihal Grameno. The in-game bust is original stylized geometry informed by [Kel Marubi's historical portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mihal_Grameno.jpg), not a reproduction or a scan of Papa's sculpture. No pixels from the portrait or current monument ship. Orientation, dimensions, and stone pedestal are authored approximations. The existing map pin is unchanged and remains the only memorial pin.
