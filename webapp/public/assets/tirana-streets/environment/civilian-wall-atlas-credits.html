Tirana Streets · civilian wall atlas

Download the 512 × 512 atlas. This atlas is a representative surface library for civilian building walls, not a survey or a photograph of every modeled property. Crops contain no people, signs or proprietary street-view imagery.

  • Brick and blue-painted plaster: JoraK, Villa on the entrance of Tefta Tashko Koço street and Building on the corner of Durrësit / Mihal Duri, both photographed 16 Nov 2018, both CC BY-SA 4.0. Adaptations rectify perspective, crop, partially normalize diffuse falloff, resize and combine the cropped wall surfaces in a compressed atlas.
  • Concrete and white stucco: Concrete Tile Facade by Charlotte Baglioni and White Stucco 02 by Dimitrios Savva, CC0. Resized and packed.
