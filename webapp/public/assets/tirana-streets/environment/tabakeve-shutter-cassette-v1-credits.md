# Ura e Tabakëve shutter cassette
Source: [Rollershutter Window 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rollershutter_window_01), MP / Poly Haven, [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
The derivative adapts only the clean version's top 21.07 cm of geometry, removes the graffiti variant, and downsamples the diffuse, normal and packed AO/roughness/metallic textures to 512 px. One 90-triangle mesh/one material.
Used as representative finish for two existing authored upper shutter covers on an unnamed building shell. This is not a survey of the building, replacement signage, or a new entrance. No change to windows, footprint or collision.
