# National flag artwork

Source: [hampusborgos/country-flags](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags),
commit `c09927e63705529bbf59ca6684cd9b23225dddad`.
Upstream identifies its Wikimedia-derived flag artwork as public domain.
The SVG artwork is distributed unchanged; `sources.json` records each download
URL, SHA-256 and original width/height ratio. Switzerland and Vatican City retain
square proportions. Albania retains its actual double-headed eagle artwork.

Cloth deformation and pole placement are original presentation code. Their
positions are estimates on the mapped building exterior, not surveyed flag mounts.
