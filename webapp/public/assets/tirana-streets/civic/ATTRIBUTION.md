# Civic NPC

`edi-rama-stylized.glb` adapts the bundled Quaternius **Ultimate Modular
Characters** suit mesh, skeleton and animations, licensed **CC0-1.0**.

- Original author/source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Audited original source: `assets-source/tirana-agent/suit-original.gltf.gz`
- Rebuild: `python3 webapp/scripts/build-civic-npc.py`

The model is a stylized artistic approximation: grey hair/eyebrows and short
grey facial hair on the original skin vertices, a charcoal suit, white shirt
and a taller runtime body scale. It is not an exact likeness,
scan, or a modification of Turi. It reuses the same shared humanoid animation
and limb-IK framework as the other game characters.

The itinerary and wait durations are invented for gameplay. Surrel and Rinas
are directional exits at the current playable road network, not real travel
information, private residences, or a surveyed airport-terminal entrance.

Vehicle models and police uniforms are reused from `vehicle-collection` and
`albanian-forces`; their individual credits and licenses continue to apply.
