The GLB is stored unchanged. Air Hockey separates its table, two mallets and puck at load time, preserves the table UVs and textures, fits the model to the existing gameplay coordinates, and adjusts material lighting. The moving pieces retain the supplied geometry with selectable gameplay colors. Attribution is also available in the game's settings.

This directory and the loader in `src/components/airHockey/` belong only to Air Hockey. Pool Royal keeps its own models, geometry, materials and configuration.
