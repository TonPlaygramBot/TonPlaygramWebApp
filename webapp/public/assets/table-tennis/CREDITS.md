## New athletes — CC0

Quaternius, **Universal Base Characters, Standard** (free edition):
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters

Source meshes: Superhero_Male_FullBody and Superhero_Female_FullBody, plus Hair_Buzzed and Hair_Buns. See `Quaternius-LICENSE.txt`, copied from the downloaded pack. These are two base models; Adrian/Luca and Maya/Nadia share their respective base mesh with different sports-kit colors. They are stylized anatomical humans, not scanned or photorealistic people.

Modifications: merged hair, removed unused animations/morph targets and auxiliary textures, resized textures with alpha preserved, welded and quantized geometry. Preview meshes are additionally simplified. Sports-kit colors and skeletal paddle poses are generated by the game. No paid Quaternius models are included.
