# Bowling material sources

The four production assets are the files listed below. All were downloaded from the official Poly Haven CDN and their sizes and MD5 hashes match the official public API metadata. JPEGs were fully decoded to verify integrity.

## Material

[White Maple Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_maple_veneer), by Jenelle van Heerden. [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Light natural maple, fine subtle figure, smooth surface. This is continuous veneer; the lane should supply narrow strip seams and modest per-board color/UV variation. The official physical tile width is 1 m. Grain runs predominantly horizontally in image space (texture U). Map U along lane length, or rotate all maps together by 90 degrees.

## Environment

[Billiard Hall](https://polyhaven.com/a/billiard_hall), photography Greg Zaal, processing Jarod Guest. [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Interior with warm overhead fixtures and soft window light. HDR is equirectangular RGBE, 1024 x 512; intended for environment lighting/reflections.

Total production assets: 12592759 bytes (12.59 MB decimal).

Use the color map as color data and normal/roughness as non-color data. The normal map uses OpenGL tangent-space convention. The material is a natural veneer scan, so a polished bowling surface needs a clear glossy finish in the renderer.

Exact direct download URLs and hashes are in asset-manifest.json. Preview JPGs and polyhaven-*.json files are research intermediates and are not needed in the Site.

## Authored glTF equipment

The original models in `webapp/public/assets/royal-lanes/models` are generated by `scripts/generate-models.mjs`: a smooth 381 mm ten-pin with enamel and neck bands, a drilled 15 lb ball with three geometric finger bores, and a batched five-lane club interior. No external model license is required. glTF 2.0 uses physical materials with clearcoat. Ten-pin scoring and lane proportions follow [USBC scoring](https://bowl.com/keeping-score) and [lane guidance](https://bowl.com/reading-the-lane).

## TonPlaygram integration

The human bowler reuses the existing `webapp/public/assets/pool-royale/readyplayer.me.glb` asset and its original materials. Its rig is posed for bowling; no external character download occurs during gameplay. Kickback walls were moved outside the gutters to match physical side-pin trajectories. The original alley materials, pin and ball models, and 4K/2K textures are preserved.
