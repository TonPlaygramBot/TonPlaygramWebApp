# Bowling Royal assets

- Lane/object layout and renderer are adapted from the user-supplied bowling prototype.
- `studio.hdr`: Studio Small 09 by Poly Haven, CC0.
- `wood*.jpg`: Oak Veneer 01 by Poly Haven, CC0, 1K derivatives supplied by Poly Haven.
  Asset license: https://polyhaven.com/license
- `bowler.glb`: the Ready Player Me avatar used by the supplied prototype and the
  existing Chess inventory, copied from the Three.js example URL. The GLB declares
  “Ready Player Me” copyright. It is not claimed to be CC0. The project's existing
  Ready Player Me commercial-use permission must cover this asset before a
  commercial release; no additional asset license is conferred by this PR.
- `cover.webp`: original generated artwork for Bowling Royal.
- Ball/pin meshes, fallback character, procedural textures, limb animation and
  synthesized audio are code-generated.

Exact source URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in `sources.json`. All game
assets are served locally; play does not depend on those third-party hosts.
