## Objective
Pocket all balls from your assigned group (solids or stripes), then legally pocket the 8-ball.

## Turn basics
- A legal pot of only solids or only stripes assigns that group, including on the break. Potting both groups leaves an open table open.
- Hit your own group first while any remain; on an open table, first contact must be a ball other than the 8.
- A legal own-group pot keeps your turn. An opponent-only pot or a legal shot without a pot passes the turn.
- A shot without a pot must send a ball to a rail after first contact. The opening shot uses this same rule.

## Common fouls
- No ball is hit first.
- Cue ball scratched (pocketed).
- Wrong first contact or no rail after contact on a shot without a pot.
- Fouls give the opponent ball in hand anywhere on the table.

## The 8-ball
Pool Royal uses the reference game's simplified 8-ball rules:

- On an otherwise legal shot, potting the 8 before your group is clear respots the 8 and gives your opponent ball in hand.
- With a group assigned, clearing its last ball and the 8 in one legal shot wins.
- Potting the 8 on a foul respots it if a group is assigned and another object ball remains. Otherwise the shooter loses.
