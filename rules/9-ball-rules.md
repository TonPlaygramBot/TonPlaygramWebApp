## Objective
Legally pocket the 9-ball. Shots must contact the lowest numbered ball first.

## Legal shot
Hit the lowest-numbered ball first; then either pocket a ball or drive any ball to a rail.

A legal pot keeps your turn. A legal 9-ball pot wins immediately, including a combination or the break. A legal shot without a pot passes the turn.

## Fouls
- Failing to hit the lowest-numbered ball first.
- Cue ball scratch.
- No rail after first contact on a shot without a pot.

Fouls give the opponent ball in hand anywhere on the table. A 9-ball potted on a foul is respotted; other potted balls remain down.

Pool Royal uses the reference game's simplified rules: the break follows the same contact/rail requirement as other shots, and there is no three-foul loss or push-out.
