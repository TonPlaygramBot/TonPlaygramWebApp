# Ura e Tabakëve: walkable cobbled deck
[Bashkia Tiranë](https://tirana.al/pika-interesi/ura-e-tabakeve-6924) documents the 2.5 m river-stone walkway; [TRT / Anadolu's photograph](https://www.trthaber.com/foto-galeri/arnavutluktaki-osmanli-eseri-tabakhane-koprusu-tum-islevselligini-koruyor/67574.html) shows the low masonry edges and pedestrian use. No photograph is bundled; curb dimensions and cobble pattern are authored.
The existing bridge already supported centerline traversal, but its visible top consisted of overlapping boxes standing above the motor's surface. A single continuous cobbled top shares section elevations with the walking motor across the bridge and both approaches. The curb has local capsule collision and does not close either entrance. The bridge position, underlying street/race routes, and sidewall imagery remain unchanged.
