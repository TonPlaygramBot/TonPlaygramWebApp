# Shared Lana channel finish

Street-level reference: [European Investment Bank field photograph](https://www.eib.org/files/publications/country/the_eib_in_the_western_balkans_2021_en.pdf) shows the characteristic narrow concrete-lined channel, grass above it, and subdued water. [51N4E's Lana Bridge project](https://51n4e.com/projects/lana-bridge/) provides views of Lana's central river corridor. These are visual references only; no photograph or material is copied. Retained center lines are OpenStreetMap-derived (ODbL); finish, water color and wet band are authored interpretations, not a hydrological survey.

Both landscapes use one visual staging module. Water reaches the point at which it meets each existing sloped concrete wall, with 3.5 cm hidden overlap. Previously the water stopped at the foot of the slope, leaving artificially bare strips even where the visual water datum was above the foot. The channel's centerline, outer banks, bed elevation, original water datums (-2.85 racing / -2.15 story), jet skis, bridges, race routes, collision, and navigation remain unchanged. Water widens only under that already cut channel, not across the elevated public realm.

A low-cost local longitudinal ripple and a narrow muted waterline darkening replace the grid-like normal waves. No new image request, reflection camera, render pass or per-channel draw call is added.
