# Tirana old-town visual pass

The public exterior between Ushtari i Panjohur, Rruga e Dibrës and Pazari i Ri uses the existing city frame. The Unknown Soldier and Sulejman Pasha sculptures retain their mounted geometry and existing OSM anchors. No route, collider, checkpoint, mission or original OSM footprint is changed.

- **Pazari i Ri:** [AADF's realised project/photograph](https://www.aadf.org/sq/project/tid-tirana/), [Bashkia's street-level photo](https://tirana.al/artikull/pazari-i-ri) and [EU Mies Awards' completed glass pavilion](https://eumiesawards.com/heritageobject/new-baazar-in-tirana-restoration-and-revitalization/) confirm the light open sides, dark steel and blue-coated angular roof. The visual 24 m generic block on [way 623438669](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/623438669) becomes a dual pitched glazed canopy in original geometry. Terracotta setts use an authored variation and no image. Stall displays are visual interpretations, not a survey or inventory; exact dimensions and roof pitch remain unmeasured. Public exterior only.
- **9-Katëshet:** [geo-labelled public street photographs](https://wikimapia.org/11636672/Pallatet-9-kat%C3%ABshe) show the pale blocks, repeated balcony bands and individual awnings; capture dates are unverified. Only mapped ways [682795212](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/682795212), [682795153](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/682795153), [682795152](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/682795152), [470177345](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/470177345) receive the custom façade. Nine levels and the existing height are preserved; widths, awning positions and exact paint are artistic interpretations.
- **Preservation:** OSM source data is unchanged. Only shared ReferenceFacades suppresses the generic opaque shell; the simulation retains prior collision behaviour, so no new navigable interior is implied. No reference images are included or fetched at runtime.
