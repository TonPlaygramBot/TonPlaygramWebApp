# Petro Nini Luarasi / Todi Shkurti

Source geometry and mapped storey counts: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL. The taller Petro Nini/Dalip Zavalani wing [620369881](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/620369881) is mapped as nine levels, separately from the three-level bakery and KMY podium [620371610](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/620371610). The local request describes ten floors and a basement, but the available reference does not establish which outline should be changed. Both original heights and footprints therefore remain, as do their collisions. The original visual suggestion of stairs descending to the bakery from street level is not a traversable basement.

[Bashkia Tiranë](https://tirana.al/njesia-administrative/njesia-nr-2/info) confirms the district office on Petro Nini Luarasi. [Century 21 West](https://www.century21albania.com/en/office/century%2021%20west.html) confirms its own address, but its office picture is a logo, not a dated exterior. The bakery below the office is user-provided local placement; KMY and Vjosa retain their [existing](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/11643279517) [mapped](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/12412793811) anchors. [The merchant on Wolt](https://wolt.com/en/alb/tirana/restaurant/motta-garden) lists Margott Garden (formerly Motta Garden) on Petro Nini Luarasi, and [the address listing](https://restaurantguru.com/Motta-Tirana) corroborates no. 14. The pin lies outside the mapped neighboring shells; the storefront/terrace footprint is unconfirmed. The similarly named [Cafe Motta on Rruga Ali Demi](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1307477073) is a different mapped location and was not reused. No storefront placement was invented, and no exact KMY/Byrektore exterior was verified.

The [January 2024 Tirana municipal engineering report](https://prokurimetransparente.al/tenders/raport_teknik/171742244355243rap.pdf) shows the study block on p. 12 and includes neighborhood field photos on pp. 10–11, with small paving units, frontage shutters, parked vehicles and multi-storey façades. These are not photographs of an as-built 2026 condition. Use only as visual context: compact paving pattern, muted neutral façades and trim. [Todi Shkurti](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/174365988) retains existing widths, curb/collision and route topology; authored asphalt and paver treatment is limited to the exact mapped name. Trim attaches to one existing street-facing exterior on each selected shell: [Todi Shkurti 2](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/736721581), [Todi Shkurti 4](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/682723386), [Kompleksi Grand](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/548100908), and the Petro Nini wing above. No source imagery is redistributed.
