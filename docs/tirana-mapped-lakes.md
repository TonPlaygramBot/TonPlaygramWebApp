# Tirana's two separate lakes

Both were already present in the runtime's typed OSM `waterAreas`, including as water restrictions in gameplay, but neither world renderer drew those polygon records. The new shared visual layer renders the two existing records only: Liqeni Artificial i Tiranës at Parku i Madh ([way/249196321](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/249196321)) and Liqeni i Thatë ([way/466431832](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/466431832)). It does not extrapolate inundation to a basin, connect lakes, or modify a road, race route, or collision. The visual y offset follows the established city-rendering convention, not a vertically measured water line.

- **Liqeni Artificial:** [Bashkia Tiranë's Grand Park page](https://tirana.al/pikat-e-interesit/atraksione-ne-tirane/parku-i-madh-i-liqenit-te-tiranes), its [mobility guide with existing paths](https://tirana.al/uploads/2021/10/20211025140316_strolling-tirana-guide_albanian-translation_edited.pdf), and [state audit photography](https://klsh.org.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Buletini-vjetor-anglisht.pdf) show the park's public shoreline and subdued olive-green water. Existing mapped paths remain on the land; no uniform edge promenade was assumed.
- **Liqeni i Thatë:** [the municipality](https://tirana.al/pikat-e-interesit/rrethinat-e-tiranes/liqeni-i-thate) records a separate approximately 6 ha lake. The [AADF February 2024 field study, pages 4–5](https://www.aadf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/H__240212_Liqeni_i_Thate_Technical_Report_V1-1.pdf) documents a lake beside the dam and gives its distinct footprint south of the highway. It is rendered only within the existing mapped water outline, not as a new permanent extent or surveyed current level. No studied or proposed pedestrian link is introduced.

Each lake receives one low-cost mesh with wind-driven ripples and a bank-relative shallow color field derived locally from its exact outline. The tints are original art direction, not satellite estimates of depth or turbidity. No third-party photo, texture, or reference image is included. Geographic evidence © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
