# Business signs visible from the street

The two game adapters use the already-shared StreetLifeLayer. Ten reviewed, previously mapped tenants now have high-mounted, double-sided signs readable parallel to the street, beside their existing front-facing fascia. These are original typeset plates, not copies of logos or replicas of verified hardware. Source IDs and supporting locations appear in `verifiedStorefrontBoards.mjs`, including Rruga e Durrësit, Muhamet Gjollesha / 21 Dhjetori, Pazari i Ri, Bardhyl, Komuna e Parisit, Astir and Todi Shkurti. The Bardhyl pizza shop relies on the stored OSM record; on-site placement and artwork have not been independently photographed.

Only the matching tenant ID, building and name may receive a plate. No sign is inferred from a nearby operator, no price/sponsorship/directional claim is made, and otherwise unverified freestanding city panels stay neutral. No third-party artwork is added. Existing race clearance, building/road footprints, navigation and collision are unchanged. The 3.64 m bottom edge is purely decorative and the short fixture stays within the existing route-clearance footprint.

Plaques reuse the existing sign atlas and box batch in central areas. Labels-only neighbourhood rendering gets at most one bounded box draw. Trade-only overlays have no separate signage. The source data remain unchanged; coordinates are existing mapped/inferred storefront positions, not a new survey.

## Coverage and omissions

The renderer now gives the existing category-matched façade signs restrained palettes and readable proportional lettering: café, restaurant, bar, bakery, confectionery, market, pharmacy, barber, hotel, bank and mall. It preserves the registry names and separately authored façade art, and does not add licensed logos or dimensions from generic photos.

The exact starting city snapshot has 348 central storefronts, 1,486 neighbourhood storefronts, no separate eastern storefront rows, and 407 large-building identities (231 hotels, 172 banks, four malls). These are 2,241 unique mapped IDs; 2,218 are inside playable bounds before any race or viewing cull. That count is registry coverage, not a count of newly installed signs, branded art, or verification of present trading status. Some rows have category-only source labels, and the data are not a comprehensive current business directory.

Of 718 audited central omissions, 304 do not tie to an unambiguous eligible building, 294 do not tie to a suitable main-street façade, 88 are not verified at street level and 32 overlap another storefront assignment. Those remain omitted; billboard slots do not infer sponsorship from nearby tenants. Category styles use original lettering; the ten separately reviewed projecting placards in this change are not claims that these physical fixtures exist at the branch today. No legacy logo assets are added or copied. Existing hardware lacking a verified current exterior is not used as evidence for new relief models.

Sign atlas allocation stays within 4,096 pixels in both dimensions at current and tested future row counts without dropping labels. Normal/battery selection limits and the existing ground facade and road clearance are unchanged.
