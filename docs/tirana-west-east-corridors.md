# Durrësit, Mine Peza, Bardhyl and Brryli streetscape pass

The two games use the same road and pavement stream. New treatments change only the existing material attribute and shader: no extra meshes, texture fetches, draw calls, colliders, lanes or changed race routes. Paving palettes, scale and signs of wear are authored, not a pavement or construction survey.

|---|---|---|
