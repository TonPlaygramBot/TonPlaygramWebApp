# Rruga e Kavajës: perimeter trees and Square 21

- Street-tree morphology is interpreted from the [Universiteti Polis street photographs](https://press.universitetipolis.edu.al/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Vilat_T3_compress.pdf), [Wikimedia's dated street photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category%3ARruga_e_Kavaj%C3%ABs), and [municipal landscaping staff's posts](https://al.linkedin.com/in/liri-qoshi-6685a5a3). Existing selected mapped nodes have an author-estimated, rounded broadleaf display rather than a generic narrow procedural tree. Species and measured heights were not established. The node IDs, positions, existing estimated dimensions and collision data do not change.
- To prevent duplicates, the [OSM west corridor map extract](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=19.790,41.320,19.806,41.326) was compared to both the base canopy registry and the independently mounted City Completion tree layer. The November 27, 2026 pull included 441 natural=tree nodes overall. All 83 apparently missing, physically clear candidate stems within 26 m of the named road were already represented in the second layer by ID or nearby coordinates, so none were added. Only existing non-cypress generic mapped stems within 19 m of the named avenue and clear of the roadway/buildings receive the morphology change. These sources do not confirm a planting survey of the full corridor. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- Two existing Square 21 building shells at [659804576](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/659804576) and [1127816735](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1127816735) now use original cladding, glazing and restrained vertical greenery inspired by the [FAB architect project](https://fab.al/en/square-21/), [XPLAN studio 2025 award photographs](https://www.theplan.it/eng/award-2025-large/square-21-in-tirana-sustainable-mixed-use-complex-with-bold-architecture-and-green-facades-xplan-studio) and [construction specification](https://arlis.al/projekti/square-21-tirane). The two buildings already had mapped eleven-level heights in game; no footprints, heights or collisions change.
- Delijorgji already has nine individually modeled mapped buildings and the Arrow Building has its own distinct reference profile. A new listing is not sufficient to match an unnamed building to an exact outline, so no unconfirmed addresses, extra buildings or planted rows are fabricated. No source photographs are bundled.
