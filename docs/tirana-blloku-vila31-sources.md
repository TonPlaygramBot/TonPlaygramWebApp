# Blloku, Myslym Shyri and Vila 31

- Vila 31: NeM Architectes' [renovation gallery](https://www.nemarchitectes.com/tirana) includes Ph. Funke's January 2025 garden view and the aerial view. The [16 January 2025 opening report](https://kryeministria.al/en/newsroom/celet-rezidenca-artistike-vila-31-x-art-explora-me-ambiente-te-hapura-kulturore/) and [operator](https://www.artexplora.org/en/residences-dartistes-tirana) confirm the current cultural use. The [Architecture Fund archive](https://tiranatriennale.com/pages/building.html?id=vila-31-x-art-explora) records Ismail Qemali 31, Blloku.
- The authored render has cream fascia, pale green/peach stucco, yellow frames, clear story bands, white railings and banded stone piers. Only [the actual historic villa shell](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/256162004) receives these motifs. The nearby same-name POI on way 470621735 is a different café, not the Art Explora shell. The existing footprint, roof mass, unmeasured game height and collision are preserved; numbers, profiles, and orientation of individual architectural bays are estimates. This models the visible cultural site only and adds no interiors, tunnels or access logic. No source pixels embedded.
- Blloku: dated [March 2025 Mapillary street views of Abdyl Frashëri](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rruga_Abdyl_Frash%C3%ABri_(Tirana)), [municipal Çoko frontage reference](https://tirana.al/pika-interesi/coko) and [Blloku Cube architect gallery](https://www.stefanoboeriarchitetti.net/en/project/blloku-cube/) are visual context. Existing mapped named road segments within the district get warm staggered pavers and a restrained asphalt finish. Generic names repeated elsewhere cannot pick up this district treatment. This does not rebuild the Cube or add unverified facilities.
- Myslym Shyri: **references, not textures**: [July 2025 street-front photograph](https://www.century21albania.com/en/property/4628885/shitet-dyqan-buze-rruges-se-myslym-shyrit-irg118072.html?share=yes), [2022 frontage showing circular paving](https://www.shpi.al/prona/objekte-biznesi/me-qera/dyqan-me-qera-ne-rr-quot-myslym-shyri-quot-qera-69429.html), and [January 2026 listing with tree-lined street](https://duashpi.al/prona/697729fb4b1d4574cb037aa2/dyqan-per-shitje-ne-myslym-shyr-buze-rruge.html). A procedural concentric motif is drawn on the existing pavement only, with a distance fade, preserving the existing road network, lane/footway geometry, planting and racing lines. It is not a measured match at every storefront.
