## Observed visual details

The municipality's bridge photo shows an unguarded humpback cobbled deck, mottled ochre/brown masonry, lighter arch rings, a sunken grass/masonry setting and black roadside railings. Background apartment facades have strong coral/red and blue accents. Preserve the bridge's pedestrian scale rather than using it as the modern vehicular bridge.

The Grand exterior photograph shows pale gray/pistachio walls, light lime floor bands, magenta-red window borders, rounded projecting balconies, curved enclosed glazing bays, dark metal balustrades, striped beige awnings, visible air conditioners and timber rooftop pergolas. These are reference cues for the pictured phase, not verified features of every block called Grand.

The Sky bar photograph published by the platform manufacturer shows dark radial ceiling beams, inward-tilted perimeter glazing with narrow mullions, a round black central counter, mustard upholstered bar stools, dark brown chairs, black tables and charcoal flooring. The operator independently confirms the rotating venue; the public images do not establish a complete interior floor plan.

## Fidelity and implementation limits

- The exact bakery identity and its facade beneath the requested Century 21 location were not independently verified. The user's relative placement can guide a generic byrektore, with precise positioning treated as an approximation.
- Larger roadside trees and smaller trees beside both Lana banks follow the requested visual direction. No complete, current tree survey or facade inventory was obtained.
- Rooftop pools are verified for the hotels listed above. These sources do not verify pools on Plaza, Arena or Downtown One; do not present invented tower pools as real amenities.
- Stairs, elevator journeys, accessible interiors, rooftop aircraft, binoculars, parachutes and the hidden weapon are gameplay additions. Their presence and layouts must not be described as verified real building features.
- Unknown building details, heights and entrance positions remain approximations. Named locations alone do not demonstrate that every surrounding building has been reconstructed.
- This document makes no claim about completed building counts, mission counts, coverage or performance. Report those only from the implemented data and actual verification results.
- Reference photos were inspected for modeling guidance only. No downloaded photograph is embedded or redistributed by this document, and no image reuse permission is implied.
