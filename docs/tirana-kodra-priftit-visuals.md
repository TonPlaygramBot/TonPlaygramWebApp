# Kodra e Priftit exterior

The artwork follows the eastern Tirana neighborhood in Njësia 3, not the road of the same name in Durrës. [Bashkia Tiranë](https://tirana.al/njesia-administrative/njesia-nr-3/info) documents reconstruction at Dishët; the existing mapped observation is preserved instead of assuming a date for resurfacing of every segment.

- Exact existing OSM ways: Disha [45029584](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/45029584), [184324924](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/184324924), [1484033050](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1484033050), Seit Gega [184324915](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/184324915), Vilson Blloshmi [184330307](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/184330307). Asphalt areas keep asphalt; Disha's separately tagged compacted segment has mineral aggregate color and dusty shoulders. No new sidewalk or street polygons, markings, changes in widths or paving where the source has no matching tag.
- Low exterior plaster and subdued warm/pale wall tones reflect [the local Century 21 hillside photo](https://www.century21albania.com/property/4163841/kodra-e-priftit-shesim-vile-2-kateshe-everest107224.html) and [the photographed Seit Gega residential context](https://duashpi.al/en/property/688c950c2117bd1c7a0a5184/-apartment-21-for-sale-in-kodra-e-priftit-seit-gega-street-tirana.html) as an architectural family, not the exact street-facing paint of any individual property. Existing licensed plaster atlas, no additional texture. Existing mapped low-rise heights, roofs and window rows stay unchanged; specified materials and colors win.
- Terrain is still flat in playable coordinates: the existing DEM spans approximately 149–175 m above sea level on the named roads, but the shared urban ground datum is deliberately level. Moving physics, road and collision surfaces together is a separate geography task; this rendering change does not simulate or claim the real gradient.

No reference photos are copied, embedded or fetched at game runtime. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
