# Tirana regional detail references

These sources guide original game geometry. They do not establish surveyed facade dimensions. Photographs are references only and are not redistributed as textures. Existing OpenStreetMap footprints, road axes, elevations and height provenance stay unchanged.

| Area | Reference | Application and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kompleksi Grand | [Wikimapia location and building photographs](https://wikimapia.org/14185230/sq/Kompleksi-Grand), [reference photograph](https://photos.wikimapia.org/p/00/01/23/50/51_big.jpg), [OSM footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/548100908) | Pale green bands, pink surrounds, curved and straight balconies, railings, shades and AC units in the recovered reviewed Blender model. Nine mapped levels retain the existing estimated 28.8 m height. |
| Njësia Administrative Nr. 2 | [Municipality information](https://tirana.al/njesia-administrative/njesia-nr-2/info), [OSM footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1227869701) | Existing yellow facade and barred windows, with projecting cornice and plinth. The existing two-storey 7 m estimate remains an estimate. |
| Qyteti Studenti | [WBIF dormitory refurbishment](https://www.wbif.eu/news-details/energy-efficient-dormitories-inaugurated-albania), [municipal dormitory refurbishment](https://tirana.al/artikull/rikonstruktohen-godinat-9-dhe-10-ne-qytetin-studenti-perfitojne-600-studente) | Repetitive window bays, renovated surfaces, gutters and AC detailing. The existing mapped campus geometry and specific campus facades are retained. |
| Farkë | [Residential listing](https://kerko360.al/listing/vila-ne-shitje-farke-429122), [Lakeshore design reference](https://lakeshore.al/villas/) | Low-rise house proportions, plaster, balconies, porches and roof drainage. Lakeshore proposals are design references, not evidence that a mapped building exists. |
| Surrel | [Three-storey residential listing](https://duashpi.al/prona/68e4d031641db658410ac346/vile-3-kateshe-per-shitje-ne-surrel.html), [OSM locality](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/728423562) | House typology and facade rhythm. Individual unverified facades remain authored interpretations. |
| Depo e Ujit | Frozen OSM acquisition in `assets-source/tirana-urban` and seven `reservoir_covered` polygons in the neighbourhood data | Add low coping around mapped reservoir perimeters, with nearby residential details. No invented reservoir heights or utility interiors. |
| Grand–Pjetër Budi / Flabina | [Historical Flabina location](https://wikimapia.org/8081920/Kolegji-Flabina) | Improve mapped surrounding buildings. The precise current Flabina school footprint and facade are unverified; no nearby building is relabelled as the school. |
